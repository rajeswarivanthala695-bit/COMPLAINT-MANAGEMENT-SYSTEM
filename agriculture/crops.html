Major Agricultural Crops

🌾 Rice

Rice is an important food crop grown extensively in many parts of India.

🌽 Maize

Maize is used as food, animal feed and as a raw material for industries.

🥜 Pulses

Pulses such as red gram and black gram are important sources of protein.

☕ Coffee

Coffee is an important plantation crop, especially in the hill regions of India.

🍅 Vegetables

Vegetables provide important vitamins, minerals and nutrients.

🍎 Fruits

Fruits such as mango, banana and citrus crops contribute to nutrition and income.

Green amaranth

Green amaranth is a very healthy,it is rich in iron, calcium,vitamins and fiber,which helps to improve blood and digestion and good for daily diet.

Red amaranth

Red amaranth is a nutritious leafy vegitable with deep red leaves. It is rich in iron and antioxidants and is used to make healthy and testy curries.

Pepper

Pepper is used to add spicy flovor to food,improve digestion and fight cold & cough.
