About Agriculture

What is Agriculture?

Agriculture is the practice of cultivating crops, raising animals and producing food and other agricultural products.

It includes activities such as farming, irrigation, harvesting, horticulture, animal husbandry and food processing.

It supplies raw matireals to industries. major crops includes rice,wheat,maize,cotton and sugarcane.

Agriculture proveds employments to many peoples it countributes to a coutrys economics development.

Modern agriculture also focuses on soil health, efficient water use, biodiversity and climate-resilient farming.

Modern agriculture uses machines improves seeds,fertilizers,and technologes sustainable agriculture helps protect soil,water and the environment.

Major Areas of Agriculture

🌾 Crop Farming

Production of rice, wheat, maize, pulses, vegetables and fruits.

🐄 Animal Husbandry

Raising cattle, goats, sheep, poultry and other livestock.

🌿 Horticulture

Growing fruits, vegetables, flowers and plantation crops.
