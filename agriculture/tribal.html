Tribal Agriculture

🌿 Araku Valley

Araku Valley is located in the Eastern Ghats of Andhra Pradesh and is known for its green hills, forests and coffee plantations.

Agriculture and plantation crops are important to communities living in the hill regions. Traditional knowledge and local farming practices are part of the region's rural life.

Araku is also associated with Arabica coffee produced by tribal farmers.
